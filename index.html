prologue

Kataware-doki, but make it us

You know that magic hour in Your Name, when the sky turns that impossible orange-pink and the line between two people just... dissolves? That's what this whole year has felt like with you 💗 I didn't have a gift to give you this time, so instead of buying something, I stayed up building you a whole little world. Scroll through it slowly, like a comet passing once in a lifetime ✨ There's a game waiting for you, and a little surprise at the very end too 😋

chapter one

Two names, tangled by fate

hum dono pehle best friends the, koi plan nahi tha, koi script nahi thi, bas itna tha ki hum ek dusre ko baar baar text karte rehte the, milte rehte the, aur baaki sab chhodke bhi ek dusre ke liye time nikaal lete the😭 mujhe pata hi nahi chala kab yeh sab shuru hua fr fr, sab kuch itna normal tha ki 'friend' wala tag kab peeche chhoot gaya, pata hi nahi chala🫂 ab peeche dekhta hun toh lagta hai wo saare boring, normal se din hi actually hamari shuruaat the, koi bada moment nahi tha, bas hum dono the✨ aur main bahut lucky feel karta hun ki tu meri hai, sab kuch kitna simple tha lekin ab sochta hun toh utna hi special lagta hai🛐

chapter two

Die With A Smile

phir ek din bas aise hi maine tujhe wo reel bheja tha 'die with a smile' wala, koi bada reason nahi tha bas vibe thi😭 thodi der baad maine khud hi ek story bhi laga di, JVKE wala gaana daal ke, jaise bina bole hi kuch keh raha hun✨ humne is baare mein kabhi ek dusre se kuch bola nahi, zarurat bhi nahi thi, dono ko pata tha ki kuch toh badal gaya hai hum dono ke beech, wahi feeling jaise toofan aane se pehle hi pata chal jaata hai🫂 wahi din tha jab pura kissa hi "us" ki taraf mud gaya tha, mujhe abhi bhi wo din bilkul clearly yaad hai💗

chapter three

The first kiss

hum dono bas aise hi baithe the ek dusre ke paas, kuch plan nahi tha, achanak tune itni softly peck kar diya ki mujhe pehle pata hi nahi chala💗 aur phir main, apni poori menace energy mein, bola "yeh tarika nahi hai" aur uske baad maine sahi se kiya, jaise actually hona chahiye tha😋 pure ek saal ki memories mein se, yehi wo choti si, bina plan ki, thodi si chaotic moment hai jise main sabse zyada baar sochta hun, agar ho sake toh isi ko loop pe jeena chahta hun😭 aaj bhi jab sochta hun toh khud hi idiot jaisa smile aa jaata hai

chapter four

Every little reason

teri smile itni angelic hai yaar, main usse words mein describe hi nahi kar sakta fr fr💗 teri cheeks sabse zyada pasand hai mujhe, itni soft hain ki bas dekhte hi rehne ka mann karta hai, tu itni pretty hai ki lagta hai kisi ne banate waqt bhi show off kiya hoga. hum dono ne bahut baar ladai ki hai, lekin tu har baar bina kuch bole mujhe maaf kar deti hai🫂 main tere liye itna clingy ho gaya hun, aur mujhe zaraaa bhi problem nahi hai🤗 tu jab gussa hoti hai tab bhi utni hi cute lagti hai jitni warna hoti hai✨ mujhe nahi pata kaise, but tu mujhe bina kuch kiye hi better insaan bana deti hai, bas apna hona hi kaafi hai iske liye🛐 kabhi kabhi sochta hun main itna lucky kaise hun ki mujhe tu mil gayi, duniya mein itne log hai but bas tu hi sahi lagti hai mujhe har baar😋 tu bas meri jaan hai, itna hi kehna hai😭
